# InkStrand support site

The support page and privacy policy for InkStrand, served by GitHub Pages at
https://srinigo2026-blip.github.io/inkstrand-support/ and linked from the App Store listing
as its Support URL and Privacy Policy URL.

Plain HTML and one stylesheet: no build step, no scripts, and nothing loaded from anywhere
else. The privacy policy says the site uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts, so
keep it that way, and keep the policy in step with the app - a new network call, permission
or stored value in the app means a change here too. Edit, commit and push; Pages republishes
within a minute or two.
